3. Storage in your browser

When you log in, a login session token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. The site also briefly caches the latest version number. Both are strictly necessary for the service you ask for, so no consent banner is required. Logging out removes the session token.

4. Who processes your data

We use these providers ("processors"), who handle data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: account login and database hosting.
  • Resend: sending account emails (confirmation, password reset).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and serves these pages.
  • jsDelivr: delivers a code library this site needs to log you in.
  • GitHub: hosts the installer. When you click Download, your browser gets the file directly from GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement.

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, the USA. Such transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

Account and synced data are kept until you delete your account. Deleting it from the Account page erases them from our database right away; encrypted backups roll over within about 7 days. Technical logs are kept for a short period by our providers (usually days to a few weeks). Accounts that were never confirmed may be removed after 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to restrict or object to processing. You can do most of this yourself on the Account page (change password, export your data, delete your account), or email us. We reply within one month.

You may also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk, or the authority where you live.

7. Children

Accounts are not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and database rules ensure each account can only read its own synced data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and notify account holders by email about significant changes.
